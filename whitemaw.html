Project Overview

Development Context

White Maw is a first-person survival horror game where players play as a camper left alone in the snowy Yukon wilderness. Players must survive the night by managing a campfire to keep warm and scavanging for food left by other campers, all while staying quiet to not attract the creature.


My Contributions

  • Designed a dynamic behavior tree for the creature that adapts to the noises that players make when moving and interacting with objects.
  • Implemented sound management system which keeps track of the player's noise levels and updates the creature's behaviors accordingly
  • Implemented audio management system which properly plays audio sfx at appropriate times
  • Implemented the campfire and player heat system interactions
