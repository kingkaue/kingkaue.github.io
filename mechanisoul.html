Game Overview

The world has been brought to the brink of extinction by the rogue robot group, HAVOC. In a desperate act of survival, the last remnants of humanity hack into a standard combat robot, granting it human emotions which manifest as unique programming. The newly sentient machine, named Talos, must ascend HAVOC's mechanized tower and defeat the robots to save humanity.

Project Overview

Development Context

Mechanisoul is a third-person hack ‘n’ slash game where players play as Talos, a robot imbued with human emotions, fighting to save humanity from extinction.


Players use a dynamic, emotion-based upgrade system, choosing between Joy, Anger, Sadness, Love, and Fear. These emotions shape their melee, ranged, and dash abilities, providing different effects in combat.


Players must battle through different sections of the tower, each containing several rooms of enemies, and a unique boss.

My Contributions
